The rally

How the rally works

  1. 01

    Check in

    Start at D’Marquee, Downtown East. Check-in begins at 9am.

  2. 02

    Get your clues

    On the day, you’ll get clues that reveal where to go for each pit stop.

  3. 03

    Complete your missions

    Drive to the pit stops and take on the challenges. Your rally ends when you’ve completed your missions.

Rally FAQs

Who gets first access to registration?

Members of the Automobile Association of Singapore (AAS) get first access. Guest registration details are pending.

When will I find out where the pit stops are?

You’ll receive clues that reveal the pit stops on event day.

What time does the rally end?

Your rally ends when you complete your missions. Check-in starts at 9am.
