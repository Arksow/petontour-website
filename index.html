How The Adventure Works

  1. 01

    Check in

    Start at D’Marquee, Downtown East. Check-in begins at 9am.

  2. 02

    Get your clues

    On the day, you’ll get clues that reveal where to go for each pit stop.

  3. 03

    Complete your missions

    Drive to the pit stops and take on the challenges. Your adventure ends when you’ve completed your missions.

FAQs

Who gets first access to registration?

Members of the Automobile Association of Singapore (AAS) get first access.

When will I find out where the pit stops are?

You’ll receive clues that reveal the pit stops on event day.

What time does the adventure end?

Your adventure ends when you complete your missions. Check-in starts at 9am.
